Since the 1800s

Not invented.
Inherited.

The guling is the long bolster in nearly every Indonesian bedroom — the one you've hugged since childhood. Before air-conditioning, it did quiet engineering: drape an arm and a leg over it and your limbs lift off the mattress, so air can move around your body through humid nights.

We kept that intelligence. Then we chose a better cover, a cleaner fill, and a length that actually fits an adult — and we numbered the first two hundred by hand.

Filled traditionally with kapok, a silky plant fibre. Named in Bahasa Indonesia — guling, "to roll." We credit the tradition; we don't rename it.

Before you reserve

The honest answers.

Still unsure? Email hello@tropihug.com — a human replies within a day.

Isn't this just a big pillow?
No. A pillow supports your head; a guling braces your whole side — arms, spine, hips, knees. It's the difference between propping and holding. The 22 cm diameter and 150 cm length are measured for an adult to drape over, not to prop against.
Won't I overheat hugging it all night?
The opposite is the point. The TENCEL™ Lyocell cover wicks moisture and breathes, and the shape lifts your top arm and leg off the mattress so air keeps circulating — the original reason Indonesians have slept with a guling for two centuries.
Will it take over the bed? Can two people share?
It's a personal-space comfort — one guling per sleeper. At 22 cm it sits comfortably beside you on a double without crowding a partner. Couples who both love it tend to keep one each (we won't pretend otherwise).
How firm is it?
Medium — supportive enough to hold alignment, soft enough to sink into. The kapok blend keeps its shape without going rigid. If it's not right for you, the 100-night trial has you covered.
How do I wash it?
The cover unzips and is machine-washable, cold, gentle. The inner keeps its loft for years. It genuinely gets softer with every wash, especially the French Linen.

Nothing today. Reserving holds your numbered place from the 200-piece first batch. We email once — when your guling is ready to ship this Fall — and only then do you pay the $148. Cancel anytime before that, no questions.
